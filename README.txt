Runtz landing page

Open index.html in a browser.

css/style.css  - all styles (widget + page)
js/main.js     - carousel, product data, email form
images/        - placeholder product images (replace product-1..6.svg, or edit paths in js/main.js)

Product data (names, prices) is in the P array at the top of js/main.js.
The Montserrat font loads from Google Fonts (needs internet; falls back to Arial).
